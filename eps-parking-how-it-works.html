EPS Parking — How It Works

How iplus integrates with the EPS (Electronic Parking System) — webhook-based vehicle entry/exit detection, quota & blacklist enforcement, overstay alerts, and visitor parking management.

Overview

What is EPS Parking?

EPS (Electronic Parking System) is one of three parallel parking-camera integrations in iplus (alongside Hikvision LPR and MHI). EPS cameras/sensors at the carpark entry/exit detect a vehicle's license plate and call a webhook on iplus. iplus then validates the plate against blacklists and quotas, logs the entry/exit, and fires configurable notifications to residents, visitors, and staff.

📷

Webhook is the transport

The EPS hardware/camera POSTs plate detections directly to iplus's public API (/api/eps/epsparkingadd) — no MQTT or polling involved.

🚦

Quota enforcement

Each condo configures how many resident and visitor vehicles are allowed at once (eps_resident_vehicle_allow, eps_visitor_vehicle_allow) — exceeding it alerts staff.

⏱️

Overstay & pre-overstay alerts

Vehicles that stay beyond a configured duration are flagged (is_overstay), with optional "X minutes before" pre-warnings to residents and visitors.

🌙

Daily reset

A scheduled DB event (eps_remove_vehicle) runs daily to reset counters and clear stale provisional entries per eps_reset_time.

Actors

Who is Involved?

📷

EPS Camera / Sensor (Hardware)

Carpark entry/exit camera that detects license plates and POSTs the result to iplus's webhook in real time.

🌐

iplus Webhook API

Public, unauthenticated endpoints (/api/eps/...) that receive plate detections and trigger stored procedures for validation and logging.

🏠

Resident

Gets notified when an invited visitor's vehicle arrives, when their own vehicle overstays, and can view parking history in the app.

👤

Visitor

Receives email confirmation/notifications when their pass is issued, when they arrive, and on overstay — based on driver email captured at entry.

🛡️

Staff / Security

Notified on blacklist hits, overstays, repeated vehicles, quota breaches, and unregistered vehicles exceeding grace period. Can manually mark vehicles as "out".

🏢

Property Management

Configures EPS settings per condo via EpssettingController — quotas, time ranges, notification templates, staff role mappings.

End-to-End Flow

How a Vehicle Entry/Exit is Processed — Step by Step

1
Condo configures EPS settings
Property management sets up quotas, operating hours, overstay durations, and notification templates/recipients before go-live.
Backend: backend/controllers/EpssettingController.php
Table: eps_setting — per-condo config: eps_resident_vehicle_allow, eps_visitor_vehicle_allow, eps_start_time_range/eps_end_time_range, eps_repeated_vehicle_time, eps_reset_time, staff role lists + message templates per event type
2
Vehicle approaches — camera detects plate
The EPS camera reads the plate number and immediately POSTs the detection to iplus.
Webhook: POST /api/eps/epsparkingadd
File: api/controllers/EpsController.php
Payload: condoId, unitId (optional), plate_no
Response codes: 200 = Success   301 = Plate already added
3
Plate is checked against the blacklist
Before logging the entry, the plate number is checked against the condo's blacklist.
Table: eps_parking_black_list — condo_id, unit_id (optional), plate_no
If matched → eps_parking_log.is_under_black_list = 1 and staff are alerted via staff_list_for_black_list_vehicle / staff_message_for_black_list_vehicle
4
Quota & repeated-vehicle checks run
The system checks how many resident/visitor vehicles are currently inside, and whether this plate has entered recently.
Quota: count of currently-parked vehicles by ref_type (1=resident, 2=visitor) compared against eps_resident_vehicle_allow / eps_visitor_vehicle_allow
If full → notify staff (staff_list_for_invited_visitor_exceeding)
Repeated check: same plate seen again within eps_repeated_vehicle_time minutes → is_repeated = 1, staff notified (staff_list_for_repeated_vehicle)
5
Entry is logged
A new row is written recording the entry, with all classification flags set.
Table: eps_parking_log — plate_no, in_time, in_or_out=1, ref_type (resident/visitor), ref_id (links to unit_visitor_pass for visitors), image, flags: is_repeated, is_under_black_list, is_overstay (initially 0)
6
Resident, visitor & staff are notified of arrival
If the vehicle is linked to an invited visitor, the inviting resident and configured staff roles are notified; the visitor may get an email confirmation.
Resident: notification_to_resident_for_drive_in_visitor (push), frequency per notification_to_resident_type (every time / first per day / once)
Visitor: notification_to_visitor_for_drive_in_visitor (email)
Staff: staff_list_when_visitor_arrives + staff_message_when_visitor_arrives
7
Pre-overstay & overstay monitoring (while parked)
While the vehicle remains parked, scheduled checks compare elapsed time against configured durations.
Pre-warning: xmin_prior_overstay_time minutes before the limit → resident_message_for_xmin_prior_overstay / visitor_message_for_xmin_prior_overstay
Overstay: when exceeded → eps_parking_log.is_overstay = 1, alerts via resident_message_for_overstay, visitor_message_for_overstay, staff_list_for_overstay + staff_message_for_overstay
Unregistered vehicles: time_for_not_exit_vehicle grace period, then staff_list_for_not_exit_vehicle alerted
8
Vehicle exits — camera detects plate again
On exit, the same webhook is called and the system matches it to the open entry record to close it out.
Webhook: POST /api/eps/epsparkingadd (in_or_out=0 / exit detection)
Updates eps_parking_log.out_time and out_image on the matching open entry
9
Manual overrides by staff (alternative path)
Staff can manually mark a vehicle (or all vehicles) as exited — e.g. if a camera misses an exit event.
POST /api/eps/epsvisitormarkasout — marks one vehicle's out_time
POST /api/eps/epsvisitormarkallasout — bulk end-of-day clear
Blacklist toggling: POST /api/eps/epsparkingaction (params: epsId, status)
10
Daily reset (scheduled event)
A MySQL scheduled event runs once a day to reset counters and clean up stale state per condo configuration.
Event: eps_remove_vehicle (file: sp_change/eps_remove_vehicle.sql) — runs daily, calls sync_parking_spot_remove() per condo
Behavior driven by eps_setting.eps_is_reset + eps_reset_time

Architecture

Data Architecture Overview

CONDO SETUP └─ eps_setting ├─ quotas: eps_resident_vehicle_allow, eps_visitor_vehicle_allow ├─ windows: eps_start_time_range, eps_end_time_range, eps_reset_time, eps_is_reset ├─ thresholds: eps_repeated_vehicle_time, time_for_not_exit_vehicle, xmin_prior_overstay_time └─ notification templates + staff role lists (per event type, EN/zh-SG) │ REAL-TIME WEBHOOK (camera → iplus) POST /api/eps/epsparkingadd { condoId, unitId?, plate_no } ├─ check eps_parking_black_list → is_under_black_list ├─ check open entries (repeated) → is_repeated ├─ check quota (ref_type vs eps_*_vehicle_allow) └─ INSERT/UPDATE eps_parking_log (in_time / out_time, image) │ VISITOR LINKAGE eps_parking_log.ref_id ──► unit_visitor_pass (ref_type = 2, visitor) │ ONGOING MONITORING ├─ pre-overstay alert (xmin_prior_overstay_time before limit) ├─ overstay alert (is_overstay = 1 once exceeded) └─ unregistered vehicle grace period (time_for_not_exit_vehicle) │ MANUAL / ADMIN OPERATIONS ├─ epsparkingaction (enable/disable blacklist entry) ├─ epsvisitormarkasout (close one entry) ├─ epsvisitormarkallasout (close all entries — EOD) └─ *export endpoints (CSV: log, repeated, visitor, blacklist) │ DAILY RESET (scheduled DB event) eps_remove_vehicle → sync_parking_spot_remove() per condo
Notifications

Who Gets Notified and When

🏠

Resident — Visitor Vehicle Arrived

Push notification when an invited visitor's vehicle is detected entering. Frequency configurable: every time / first per day / once only (notification_to_resident_type).

📧

Visitor — Pass Issued / Arrival Confirmation

Email sent using visitor_pass_has_been_issued_en / _zh_sg and notification_to_visitor_for_drive_in_visitor templates, with placeholders for driver name and plate number.

⏰

Resident & Visitor — Overstay / Pre-Overstay

Configurable pre-warning (xmin_prior_overstay_time minutes before limit) and overstay alerts via resident_message_for_overstay / visitor_message_for_overstay.

🛡️

Staff — Blacklist, Repeated, Overstay, Quota, Grace-Period Alerts

Each event type has its own configurable staff role list and message template: staff_list_for_black_list_vehicle, staff_list_for_repeated_vehicle, staff_list_for_overstay, staff_list_for_invited_visitor_exceeding, staff_list_for_not_exit_vehicle, staff_list_when_visitor_arrives.

Security

Access Control & Operational Safeguards

Quota Enforcement

Separate resident/visitor vehicle limits prevent overcrowding — exceeding the visitor quota immediately alerts staff.

Blacklist Checking

Every detected plate is checked against eps_parking_black_list before being logged, with immediate staff alerting on a hit.

Overstay & Pre-Overstay Alerts

Two-stage alerting (pre-warning + overstay) gives residents/visitors a chance to move their vehicle before escalation to staff.

Manual Override & Daily Reset

Staff can manually close stuck entries (single or bulk), and a scheduled daily event resets counters to prevent state drift.
